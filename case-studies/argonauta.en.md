# Argonauta

[Italiano](argonauta.md) · English · [Profile](../README.en.md)

Argonauta is an iOS exploration app I’m developing in Swift and SwiftUI. The map helps walkers follow a route. The Atlas helps them discover what they are walking past.

## One walk across three screens

A walker can start a route on the map and switch to the Atlas to read about a place, an animal or a local legend. The active route stays available when they leave the map. As they explore, they can collect cards with sources. Later, the Explorer’s Journal brings together the route, the stops and the discoveries from the walk.

One design choice was to label local legends explicitly and include their sources. A walker who opens a card can tell when the app is retelling a story about a place.

## Project status

Route tracking, the Atlas and the journal are implemented. The app is currently undergoing testing on iPhone and iPad.
